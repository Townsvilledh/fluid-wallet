Support Fluid

Fluid is a free, open-source, non-custodial wallet for every blockchain. There is no account and no sign-up: one seed phrase, no KYC, and your keys never leave your device. Fluid is free to use, and it stays that way.

Donations fund the work behind it — route testing, infrastructure, and security review. Of every donation, 80% goes to the work and 20% to upkeep. Any amount helps.
