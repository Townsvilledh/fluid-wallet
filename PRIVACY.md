# Fluid — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

Fluid is a free, open-source, non-custodial crypto wallet that runs entirely in
your browser as a progressive web app (PWA). This policy explains, plainly,
what data Fluid does and does not handle.

## Fluid collects nothing

- **No accounts.** Fluid has no sign-up, no login, and no user database.
- **No personal data collection.** Fluid does not collect, store, transmit, or
  sell your name, email, documents, or any other personal information. There is
  no Fluid company server holding user data.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The Fluid app contains no analytics, advertising,
  or third-party tracking code.

## Your keys never leave your device

Your seed phrase and private keys are generated on your device and stored only
in your browser's local storage, encrypted with your password. They are never
sent to Fluid or to anyone else. Anyone with your seed phrase controls your
funds — keep it offline and private. Fluid cannot recover a lost seed phrase.

## Network connections

To show balances and prices and to broadcast transactions you ask for, the app
connects directly from your device to public blockchain nodes, price sources,
and swap-routing providers. As with any internet service, those providers can
see your device's IP address and the public blockchain addresses and queries
involved in your request. Their handling of that data is governed by their own
policies. Blockchain transactions themselves are public by design.

## Usernames are public

If you claim a Fluid username, that claim is a signed public registry entry by
design — it links your username to your public wallet addresses so others can
pay you by name. Do not claim a username if you do not want that link to be
public.

## Changes and contact

The source code is public at https://github.com/Townsvilledh/fluid-wallet — you
do not have to take this policy on trust; you can read exactly what the app
does. Questions can be raised as issues in that repository.
